About MediChain

Research-grade blockchain framework for healthcare

The Research Foundation

MediChain is based on peer-reviewed research proposing a scalable cross-chain Layer-2 blockchain framework for privacy-preserving healthcare data exchange. The system combines three key technologies to overcome the limitations of existing single-chain healthcare blockchain systems.

1
Layer-2 Rollups

Aggregate 50 transactions per batch, reducing cost by 75% and increasing throughput 10x versus single-chain systems.

2
Zero-Knowledge Proofs

Verify medical data authenticity without revealing the underlying content. O(1) on-chain verification regardless of batch size.

3
Cross-Chain Relay

Triple-gate security: signature verification, ZK proof check, and nonce freshness prevent replay attacks and forgery.
