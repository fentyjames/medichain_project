What happens next
  1. Each input is SHA-256 hashed individually.
  2. A commitment is formed: hash(all_input_hashes + public_output).
  3. The proof JSON is returned — share it with verifiers or attach it to a cross-chain message.
  4. The verifier re-hashes the same inputs and confirms the commitment matches.
