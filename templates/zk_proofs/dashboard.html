ZK Proofs in MediChain
What gets proven?
  • A patient's record was created by an authorised hospital — without revealing the record's contents
  • A diagnosis hash matches the expected value — without exposing the diagnosis
  • A patient's age is above a threshold — without disclosing the date of birth
  • An insurance claim hash is included in a rollup batch — without revealing other claims
Simulation note

MediChain's ZK proofs are a simulation — proofs are structured JSON objects of hashed inputs rather than real zk-SNARK/STARK cryptographic proofs. They demonstrate the data flow and verification logic of a production ZK system without requiring heavyweight cryptographic libraries.
